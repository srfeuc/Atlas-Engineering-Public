## The amendment model — how these decisions relate to this policy

This policy holds the **current** rule; the decisions behind it are the **dated trail**. No standing rule changes by editing this policy silently.

- **To change a rule, an ADR amends it** — the ADR carries `Governing Policy: POL-XXXX`, states *"amends `POL-XXXX` R#"*, and this policy's Change Log gains a row citing that ADR + date (integrated change control, [`ADR-0054`](../Decisions/ADR-0054-Governance-Reconciliation-Promote-Policy-Shaped-ADRs.md)).
- **(C) decisions** (a rule promoted) are re-scoped to *the decision that adopted the rule* — kept, never deleted, originals in the legacy ADR snapshot.
- **(B) decisions** (a standard-in-effect) are recognised in place with a *"Doc-type: Standard-in-effect"* note — **not** renumbered.
